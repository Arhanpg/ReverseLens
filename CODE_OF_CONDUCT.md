# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want ReverseLens to be an open, welcoming and constructive project. Participants should interact respectfully and focus on technical merit, shared learning and responsible research.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Provide actionable feedback without personal attacks.
- Acknowledge contributions and cite upstream work.
- Respect project safety, privacy and authorization boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberately disrupting project infrastructure.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, credential theft, privacy invasion or abuse of third-party systems.
- Submitting malicious code, poisoned fixtures or deceptive benchmark results.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove violating content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation or take other reasonable action. Reports should be sent privately through the repository's maintainer contact route.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and public interactions where someone is representing the project.
